Currently building

Brodie.

A personal coach in your texts. No app to open.

Brodie lives in iMessage. It remembers every lift, fixes your form from the video you already film, writes your programming, and texts first when you ghost the gym. Coaching that adapts to the individual instead of the average.

The hard parts are pose analysis that finds the exact rep your form broke down, memory that holds months of training context, and a coach that earns trust one text at a time. I'm treating the body the way modern tooling treats code — instrumented, diff-able, improvable.
